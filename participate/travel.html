Travel Information

Visa & Entry Requirements

Most international visitors do not require a visa to enter South Korea for short stays. K-ETA (Korea Electronic Travel Authorization) is generally required for visa-free entry, but it is temporarily exempted for 22 countries/regions (including USA, Japan, UK, Australia, etc.) until December 31, 2026. Please check if your country is eligible before applying.

  • K-ETA Application: Official K-ETA Website
  • Q-Code (Quarantine Info): Recommended for faster entry. Q-Code System

Getting to Jeju

The conference will be held at Jeju Island. The main gateway is Jeju International Airport (CJU).

Option 1: Via Incheon (ICN) & Gimpo (GMP) - Most Common

Most international flights land at Incheon International Airport (ICN). From there, you can transfer to Gimpo International Airport (GMP) to take a domestic flight to Jeju (CJU).

  • Transfer: Take the AREX (Airport Railroad) or Limousine Bus from Incheon to Gimpo (approx. 30-40 mins).
  • Flight to Jeju: Flights from Gimpo to Jeju depart every 15-20 minutes.

Option 2: Direct International Flights

Some airlines operate direct flights to Jeju International Airport (CJU) from major Asian cities (e.g., Tokyo, Osaka, Beijing, Shanghai, Taipei, Singapore).

Transportation to Venue

From Jeju International Airport to Jeju Shinhwa World

Public Bus (Recommended)

Bus No. 151 (Express Bus)
Boarding: Gate 4 (1st Floor)
Drop-off: Jeju Shinhwa World
Time: Approx. 50 mins
Fare: ~3,000 KRW
*Cashless Bus: T-money card required

Taxi

Boarding: Taxi Stand across Gate 3
Time: Approx. 40-50 mins
Fare: ~35,000 - 45,000 KRW
(Fares may vary based on traffic and time)

Rental Car

Rental car counters are located in the arrival hall. Advance reservation is highly recommended. International Driving Permit (IDP) is required.

General Information

Currency

South Korean Won (KRW). Credit cards are widely accepted.

Electricity

220V, 60Hz. Standard 2-pin plugs (Type C/F). Adapters may be needed.

Weather (April/May)

Spring season. Mild and pleasant.
Avg. Temp: 13°C - 20°C (55°F - 68°F).
