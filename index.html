Introduction to DASFAA

About DASFAA

DASFAA is an annual international database conference, located in the Asia Pacific region, aimed at showcasing the state-of-the-art and broad range of activities in database-related research and development. It provides a forum for technical presentations and discussions among database researchers, developers and users from academia, business and industry.
